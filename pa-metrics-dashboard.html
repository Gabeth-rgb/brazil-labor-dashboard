⚠ Seasonal Reset Pattern Detected The dataset shows two distinct JRS & Skills reset cycles: Jan–Feb 2026 the population started near 0% and climbed rapidly to 98%+ by March, then reset again in early July 2026 (dropping to ~7%) before recovering. This corresponds to the annual/semi-annual IBM PBC/JRS evaluation cycles. Managers should pre-plan to clear their teams before the reset date.
✓ JRS Assessed recovers faster than Skills Proficiency Historically JRS Assessed compliance outpaces Skills Proficiency compliance during each cycle. The gap averages ~2–3 percentage points, suggesting practitioners deprioritize skill ratings after completing JRS.

🔍 Pattern Analysis & Key Findings

🚨 Hours Plan Compliance — Persistent Gap Hours Plan compliance is the lowest-performing metric across the entire dataset, starting at 69.6% in Jan 2026 and slowly improving to ~90% by mid-2026 — still the most lagging indicator. Roughly 1 in 10 practitioners consistently deviates beyond the ±10–20% threshold every week. Projects Bradesco (31 non-comp), Vale (23), Itaú (18), and Vigação Lago team (17) account for a disproportionate share of violations in the latest week.
⚠ JRS & Skills — Biannual Reset Volatility Two full reset cycles were observed within the 31-week window. Each cycle starts near 0–7% and recovers to 95–98% over ~6 weeks. The recovery rate averages +15 pp/week. Late-recovering subgroups are consistently the same each cycle — flagging a structural training or awareness gap rather than a one-time event.
⚠ No Future Assignment — Structural Bench Risk The "Upcoming Weeks With No Assignment" flag is elevated at ~82–83% flag-rate in April 2026 (meaning ~17% of practitioners had no future assignment during that period). This creates bench risk and potential billing gaps. The metric improved to ~98% by July 2026, but the April trough warrants investigation into project pipeline gaps.
✓ CV Compliance — Near Perfect Sustained CV compliance climbed from 93.4% in Jan to 99.8–100% by June 2026 and has remained above 99.5% since. This represents a clear, successful remediation campaign. The month-over-month improvement of ~1–1.5 pp/week during Feb is consistent with a targeted push.
✓ Timely Claim — Consistently Strong Timely claim compliance is the strongest metric, consistently above 96–100%. It declined slightly from 99.8% in Jan to ~96.7% by May-Jun, which may correlate with increased headcount on certain projects. McDonald's and Santander RE achieved 100% compliance in the latest week.
📊 Headcount Trend — Stable Workforce Active practitioner headcount is stable at ~1,430–1,516 per week across 31 weeks. No significant spikes or drops outside ±5% variation, confirming the dataset represents a mature, steady-state workforce population.
👥 Manager Attention Areas APARECIDA DA SILVA, MA (311 HC, 23 HP non-comp), CUSTODIO DA SILVEIRA NETO, NC (141 HC, 15 HP non-comp), and FAVERO, M (109 HC, 15 HP non-comp) have the highest absolute hours plan non-compliance counts in the latest week. These three managers together account for 53 of the total 131 HP non-compliance cases.
